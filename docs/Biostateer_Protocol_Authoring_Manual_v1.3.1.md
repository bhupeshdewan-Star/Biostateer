# Biostateer™ Enterprise Clinical Research & Biostatistics Intelligence Platform
## Version 1.3.1 — Protocol Intelligence Engine Manual

---

### 1. Automated 30-Section Protocol Compiler
Biostateer™ Version 1.3.1 integrates an advanced expert-system protocol compiler configured in accordance with the SPIRIT (Standard Protocol Items: Recommendations for Interventional Trials) guidelines. The engine generates comprehensive study protocols containing the following sections:
1. **Title Page**: Study name, registration details, active version blocks.
2. **Protocol Synopsis**: Structured summary of disease area, study objectives, and key phases.
3. **Scientific Background**: Disease context and mechanism of action.
4. **Risk-Benefit Assessment**: Safety profiles, anticipated side effects, and risk mitigation strategies.
5. **Study Objectives**: Primary, secondary, and exploratory objectives.
6. **Trial Endpoints**: Primary and secondary endpoints with formal definitions.
7. **Study Design**: Parallel, crossover, replicate, or observational trial configurations.
8. **Randomization Schema**: Block, stratified, or simple randomization algorithms.
9. **Blinding Procedures**: Double-blind, single-blind, or open-label execution protocols.
10. **Investigational Product**: Dosage, administration, and storage instructions.
11. **Inclusion & Exclusion Criteria**: Strict participant eligibility matrices.
12. **Trial Duration & Milestones**: Patient recruitment timelines, interim analysis schedules.
13. **Statistical Analysis Plan (SAP)**: Comprehensive parametric, non-parametric, ANOVA, or MANOVA schemas.
14. **Sample Size Justification**: Power calculations, alpha limits, and GMR-CV sensitivity tables.
15. **Data Monitoring Committee (DMC)**: Structure, meetings, and early termination rules.
16. **Adverse Event Reporting**: Grading, timeline, and CDSCO/USFDA safety report forms.
17. **Concomitant Medications**: Permitted and prohibited therapies.
18. **Data Management & Lock**: Spreadsheet freezes, metadata locks, audit trails.
19. **Ethical Considerations**: Declaration of Helsinki, IRB/IEC approvals, and consent forms.
20. **Patient Information Sheet**: Plain language explanations for study participants.
21. **Informed Consent Form**: Electronic signature gates, audit trail mapping.
22. **Quality Assurance**: Monitoring plans, source data verification rules.
23. **Protocol Amendments**: Control processes, version increments.
24. **Dissemination Policy**: Publication rules, authorship agreements.
25. **Funding & Sponsorship**: Financing sources, institutional roles.
26. **Conflict of Interest**: Disclosures from investigators and statistical reviewers.
27. **Subject Compensation**: Travel reimbursements, injury insurance coverage.
28. **Trial Termination Criteria**: Futility rules, safety limits.
29. **References**: Bibliography of guidelines and regulatory templates.
30. **Appendices**: Schema of procedures, schedules, and definitions.

---

### 2. Export & Custom Templates
* **Multi-Format Compilation**: Export protocols as styled DOCX files, high-fidelity printable PDFs, or clean web-ready HTML bundles.
* **Phase-Specific Recommendations**: Automatically injects regulatory clauses based on the selected study phase (e.g., Phase I dose escalation vs. Phase III multiplicity controls).
